Radio emission from active galactic nuclei (AGNs) is ubiquitous, yet the physical mechanisms behind it are not always straightforward to disentangle from observations alone. Using relativistic-magnetohydrodynamic simulations, I investigate how AGN-driven jets and winds evolve and interact with their surroundings, and how these processes shape the radio emission and observable diagnostics of AGN feedback.
